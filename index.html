About Tiny Apps

What Are Tiny Apps?

Tiny Apps are single-file, lightweight web applications designed to solve specific problems with elegance and simplicity. No bloat, no frameworks, no configuration—just pure functionality.

Each app is built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, making them instantly loadable and deployable anywhere. Perfect for developers, designers, and creators who value efficiency.

Why Tiny Apps?

  • Zero external dependencies
  • Lightning-fast load times
  • Works offline
  • Easy to customize
  • Open source & free
